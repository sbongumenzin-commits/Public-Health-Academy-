About the Academy

The Public Health Academy is a CPD-accredited institution dedicated to empowering healthcare professionals through evidence-based education, professional development, and innovative training in public health and environmental health practice.

Our Vision

  • To be a leading institution in providing professional public health education
  • Empower healthcare professionals to drive sustainable health systems
  • Improve community well‑being through life‑long learning

    • For enquiries, please email us directly at sbongumenzi.n@gmail.com.

Our Mission

To provide high‑quality, CPD‑accredited education and training that enhances the competence, confidence, and career growth of healthcare professionals through innovative and evidence-based training programmes.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence: Upholding the highest standards in training and educating.
  • Integrity: Acting ethically and with transparency in all practices.
  • Innovation: Embracing creativity and continuous improvement.
  • Impact: Transforming communities through knowledge and action.

Our Services

  • CPD-Accredited Training Programs: Comprehensive courses in public health, environmental health, and related fields, accredited for continuing professional development.
  • Professional Development Workshops: Interactive sessions designed to enhance skills and knowledge for healthcare professionals across various disciplines.
  • Public Health Symposiums: Annual events bringing together experts and practitioners to discuss the latest trends and innovations in public health.
  • Online Learning Modules: Flexible, self-paced online courses accessible from anywhere, covering specialized topics in health practice.
  • Conducting Baseline Studies and Research: Performing baseline studies and other research work for organisations and individuals to inform decision-making and development.
  • Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA): Conducting EIA for upcoming and existing structures to ensure environmental compliance and sustainability.
  • Environmental Health Services and Trainings: Providing environmental health related services and trainings to private food and OHS industries to promote safety and compliance.
  • Consulting Services: Expert advice for healthcare organizations on policy development, program implementation, and compliance.
